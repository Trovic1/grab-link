Get the Grab app

The app downloads through your own internet, so YouTube works too, in up to 1080p. Free, no account.

WindowsWindows 10 and 11
Download for Windows
  1. Open GrabSetup.exe.
  2. If Windows says it “protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. That shows for any new app that isn't from a big company.
  3. Click the Grab icon on your desktop. It opens in your browser.
AndroidAndroid 8 and newer
Download for Android
  1. Open Grab.apk from your downloads.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. In any app, tap Share → Grab, or copy a link and open Grab.

Faster ways in the browser

Auto-grab

Turn on “Auto-grab copied links”. When you come back to this tab, the link you copied downloads by itself.
